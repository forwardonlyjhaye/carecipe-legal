Questions

Is Carecipe free?

Yes. Download it free and get 200 recipes plus 15 AI actions a month for scans, searches and imports. Carecipe Plus opens all 1,322 recipes and lifts the cap, with a 7-day free trial. Cancel any time.

Do I need an account?

No. There's nothing to sign up for. Your recipes live on your phone.

How does the fridge scan work?

Take a photo of your fridge, cupboard or shopping. Carecipe lists the ingredients it sees, you tidy up anything it got wrong, and it ranks dishes by how much of each one you can already make.

Can I save recipes from TikTok and Instagram?

Yes. Tap Share on the post, choose Carecipe, and you get a clean recipe with the creator's credit kept on it. Links, pasted text and photos of cookbook pages work too.
